The Venue

Hotel Temple IN, Trichy

Located conveniently on Vayalur Road in the temple heartland of Tiruchirappalli

Warm Hospitality & Grand Celebrations

Situated in Vasan City on Vayalur Road, Hotel Temple IN provides a refined, comfortable setting for auspicious family gatherings, surrounded by the cultural heritage and divine shrines of Trichy.

  • 📍 Full Address: Hotel Temple IN, Vayalur Rd, Rettai Vaikal, Ram Nagar, Vasan City, Trichy, Tamil Nadu 620102
  • 🚗 Connectivity: Easy access from Trichy Junction Railway Station (~7 km) and Trichy International Airport (~12 km)
  • 🛕 Sacred Shrines: Close to Sri Vayalur Murugan Temple, Srirangam Ranganathaswamy Temple & Rockfort Temple
  • 🅿️ Guest Comfort: Spacious parking, air-conditioned banqueting halls, and dedicated guest reception
📍 Open in Google Maps

Travel Directions to Venue:

From Trichy Central Bus Stand or Railway Junction, proceed along Vayalur Main Road towards Rettai Vaikal. Hotel Temple IN is conveniently located in Vasan City, Ram Nagar. Auto-rickshaws, cabs, and local buses are readily available.
